    Utilization



    Utilization - measures the percentage of an agent's paid time that they spend handling customer interactions or other productive work, excluding time spent on breaks or non-productive tasks. It is calculated by dividing the total time spent on customer-related activities (like talk time, after-call work, and hold time) by their total paid hours. High utilization indicates efficiency, but a rate that is too high can lead to agent burnout and lower call quality, while a low rate may signal overstaffing.

    How to improve utilization:

    • Streamline after-call work (ACW) : Automate repetitive tasks like note-taking and follow-up emails to free up agent time.
    • Optimize scheduling : Use data to create smarter schedules that align agent availability with call volume.
    • Provide coaching and tools :Offer real-time coaching and equip agents with helpful tools to work more efficiently.
    • Leverage technology : Use AI and workforce management systems to automate routine tasks and manage agent schedules more effectively.


    How Utilization is Calculated:

      Utilization %  =            Workload Hours                  x100%

                   Total Staffed Hours (Paid)
